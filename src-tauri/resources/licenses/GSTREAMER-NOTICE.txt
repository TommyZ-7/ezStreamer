Bundled runtime: GStreamer MSVC binaries (https://gstreamer.freedesktop.org/).
Installed at build time from the official release (see docs/design.md §13.2
for the pinned version); the installer carries the DLLs the pipeline needs
(base/good/bad/ugly + D3D11/WASAPI/media-foundation plugins).
